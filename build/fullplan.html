All 35 seven-marker types, across both options in every unit — Q1 and Q2, Q3 and Q4, Q5 and Q6, Q7 and Q8, Q9 and Q10. Plus the 2-mark bank. Nothing skipped.

The trade-off, stated plainly

Full coverage of 35 types needs about 19 hours. You have 18. That is close enough to work, but only because depth is tiered rather than coverage being cut.

The honest cost: you get three reps on the 11 highest-frequency topics and one clean rep on everything else. That is recognition plus one pass, not fluency, on the long tail. It is the right shape for "no question can blindside me" — it is not the shape for "I will finish every question in 20 minutes". You are buying breadth with depth, deliberately.

If you fall behind

Compress in this order — never drop a topic entirely, downgrade its depth instead. Coverage is the whole point of this version of the plan; a topic at SKIM still lets you start the question and take method marks.

1. Downgrade these four from PASS to SKIM (saves 48 min): §20 Newton-Gregory · §21 Lagrange interpolation · §29 rank & consistency · §30 Gauss elimination.
2. Downgrade §17 Legendre and §19 IVP/BVP to SKIM (saves 24 min) — both are short riders on work you have already done.
3. Cut the DRILL topics from three reps to two (saves ~50 min across the eleven). Do this last — those are the highest-frequency questions in the paper.

Never compress: §15 CF + P.I. (everything in Unit III depends on it) · §32 eigenvalues (§33, §34, §35 all depend on it) · the 2-mark bank · Tuesday's speed run.
