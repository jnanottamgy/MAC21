MAC21 — Prediction Sheet

Numerical Techniques and Differential Equations · B.E. Semester II · Ramaiah Institute of Technology · SEE, 100 marks, 3 hours

Built from 6 papers: the Department of Mathematics Model Question Paper I (MP) · MAC21 Sep/Oct 2023 (M23) · MAC21 Jul/Aug 2024 (M24) · MAM21 Jul/Aug 2024 (MAM24, Units I–IV) · MAE21 Sep/Oct 2023 (MAE23, Units I–III) · 25MAC21 Jun/Jul 2026 (P26, partial — different scheme).
Everything off your syllabus has been stripped out — no multiple integrals, no Laplace transforms, no Beta/Gamma functions, no PDEs, no Regula-Falsi, no LU decomposition, no Weddle's rule. Only MAC21 content appears below.
How to read the repeat counts. Coverage differs by unit, so a count is always out of the papers that actually carry that unit: Units I–III → 6 papers · Unit IV → 5 papers (MAE23's Unit IV is Laplace) · Unit V → 3 papers (only MP, M23, M24 — the 2026 scheme deleted this unit). A "3×" in Unit V is therefore a clean sweep, not a weak signal.

Realistic band: 70–85 marks.

Tier 1 alone — the verbatim repeats — puts roughly 45–55 marks on the table before you have solved anything new. Tier 2 carries you to the low 80s. The gap to 100 is the one unfamiliar 7-marker per unit that no prediction can eliminate.

Two failure modes to avoid.
1. Do not revise from the 2026 paper alone. It is a different scheme (25MAC21). It never tests Cauchy, Legendre, variation of parameters, Lagrange multipliers, maxima–minima, or any linear algebra — roughly 35 marks of your syllabus.
2. Do not skip Unit V because it is taught last. It is the smallest unit (~6 techniques), the most predictable in the whole paper, and "system of ODEs by matrix method" has been the final question of the exam in 3 out of 3 papers.
If you only get a few hours. In this order:
1. The 2-mark bank — section 6, one pass. 10 marks, pure recall.
2. 1/(D+a) proof + Cauchy + variation of parameters. 18 marks in Unit III, all three near-certain.
3. Unit V end to end — Gauss-Seidel, Rayleigh, diagonalization, system of ODEs by matrix method. ~6 techniques, 20 marks.
4. Modified Euler + RK4 + Newton's law of cooling. Most of Unit II.
5. Missing terms + divided difference + grouped frequency. Most of Unit IV.
6. Expand about (1, π/2) + xy(1−x−y) + the N-R system. Most of Unit I.
