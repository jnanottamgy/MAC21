## 🚨 Scheme change: MAC21 vs 25MAC21

The June/July 2026 paper is **25MAC21 "Numerical Methods"** — a *different scheme* from
your **MAC21 "Numerical Techniques and Differential Equations"**. Its Unit II is entirely
multiple integrals (not in your syllabus) and it drops your whole linear-algebra unit.
Roughly 70% of it is still usable practice.
**Read [`SYLLABUS-CHANGE-2025.md`](SYLLABUS-CHANGE-2025.md) before using that paper.**

## ⚠️ Off-syllabus warning

Two of the four papers are **sister papers** — same institution and semester, different
course code for a different branch. Their early units overlap closely with MAC21 and make
good extra practice, but their later units are a different subject entirely:

- **MAM21 Unit V** = Partial Differential Equations (heat/wave equation, Bendre-Schmidt,
  separation of variables). **Not in MAC21.** Your Unit V is Linear Algebra.
- **MAE21 Units IV & V** = Beta/Gamma functions and Laplace Transforms.
  **Not in MAC21.** Your Units IV and V are Interpolation/Numerical Integration and
  Linear Algebra.

Do not revise from those sections.

## Paper format (identical every year)

```
100 marks · 3 hours · 5 units
Unit I → Q1 or Q2 · Unit II → Q3 or Q4 · Unit III → Q5 or Q6
Unit IV → Q7 or Q8 · Unit V → Q9 or Q10

Each question:  a) 02   b) 04   c) 07   d) 07   = 20 marks
Answer ONE full question from each unit (internal choice).
```
