Gaps — on your syllabus, missing from the notes

1. Finite-difference operator relations (session 18) — nowhere in the notes. You need Δ²yₙ = y_(n+2) − 2y_(n+1) + yₙ and ∇²yₙ = yₙ − 2y_(n−1) + y_(n−2). The 2-marker "express Δ²yₙ in terms of y values" has appeared twice, at slot Q7(a). Also worth knowing: Δ = E − 1, ∇ = 1 − E⁻¹.

2. Legendre operator relations — p7 gives the Cauchy ones (xy′ = Dy, x²y″ = D(D−1)y) then leaves the Legendre box blank. Write them in: (ax+b)y′ = aDy   (ax+b)²y″ = a²D(D−1)y   (ax+b)³y‴ = a³D(D−1)(D−2)y. Forgetting the a² is the classic way to lose this question.

3. The inconsistent case — p11 lists unique and infinite solutions but not ρ(A) ≠ ρ(A:B) → no solution. That is one of the three cases the 2-marker asks for.

4. Two techniques with no formula to memorise, so they are absent by nature but still examinable: missing terms (set the higher differences to zero) and grouped-frequency estimation (build the cumulative "less than" column first). Both are in the solutions PDF, §23 and §24.
