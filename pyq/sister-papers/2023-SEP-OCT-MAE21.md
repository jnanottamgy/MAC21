## UNIT — I  ✅ *on-syllabus*

**Q1.** a) Define maxima and minima for the function of two independent variables. (02) · b) Determine the extreme value of `f(x, y) = xy(1 − x − y)` at `(1/3, 1/3)`. (04) · c) Find the Maclaurin's series for `tan x` up to the term containing `x⁴`. (07) · d) Compute the real root of `x log₁₀x = 1.2` by Newton-Raphson, correct to three decimal places. (07)

**Q2.** a) Write the formula to find the Taylor's series expansion of functions of two variables. (02) · b) Solve the system of non-linear equations `y² + x = 7`, `x² + y = 11` at `(3.5, −1.8)` by Newton-Raphson. Carry out one iteration. (04) · c) Obtain the Taylor's series expansion of `xy + sin(xy)` about `(1, π/2)` up to second degree terms. (07) · d) Find the extreme value of `f(x, y) = x³ + y³ − 3x − 12y + 20`. (07)

## UNIT — II  ✅ *mostly on-syllabus*

**Q3.** a) Define orthogonal trajectory of a family of curves. (02) · b) A bungee jumper of mass 68.1 kg leaps from a stationary hot air balloon. Use `dv/dt = g − cv²/m`, `g = 9.8 m/s²`, `c = 0.25 kg/m`, to compute velocity for the third second of free fall by Euler's method in steps of 1 second. (04) · c) Solve `(y³ − 3xy²)dx + (2x²y − xy²)dy = 0`. *(exact/homogeneous — peripheral to MAC21)* (07) · d) An emf `e = 200e^(−5t)` is applied to a series circuit of 20 ohm resistor and 0.01 F capacitor. Find the charge and current at any time, assuming no initial charge on the capacitor. (07)

**Q4.** a) Write the steps involved in finding the orthogonal trajectories of a family of curves in **polar** form. (02) · b) Solve `(1 + 2xy cos x² − 2xy)dx + (sin x² − x²)dy = 0`. *(exact — peripheral)* (04) · c) Show that the family of curves `x²/(a²+λ) + y²/(b²+λ) = 1` is self-orthogonal, where λ is the parameter. (07) · d) Solve the IVP `dy/dx = xy + y²`, `y(0) = 1` at `x = 0.4` taking `h = 0.4` using RK4. (07)

## UNIT — III  ✅ *on-syllabus*

**Q5.** a) Define a boundary value problem with an example. (02) · b) Solve `(2x+3)² y″ + 2(2x+3) y′ − 12y = 0`  *(Legendre)*. (04) · c) Solve `(D³ − 7D − 6)y = e²ˣ(1 + x)`. (07) · d) Solve `y″ − 2y′ + 2y = eˣ tan x` by the method of variation of parameters. (07)

**Q6.** a) Define linear and nonlinear differential equations. (02) · b) If `D = d/dx` and `X = X(x)`, prove that `[1/(D+a)] X = e^(−ax) ∫ X e^(ax) dx`. (04) · c) Solve `(D² − 4D + 3)y = sin 3x · cos 2x`. (07) · d) Solve the Cauchy's LDE `x² y″ − 4x y′ + 6y = cos(2 log x)`. (07)
