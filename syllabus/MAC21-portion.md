# MAC21 — Numerical Techniques and Differential Equations

**Course:** MAC21 — Numerical Techniques and Differential Equations
**Programme:** Engineering (Computer Science)
**Source:** Session-wise lesson plan / portion sheet (page 4)
**Structure:** 5 units · 28 sessions · 1 hour per session

---

## UNIT-I — Series Expansion of Functions & their Applications
*Sessions 1–6 · 6 hrs*

| Session | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Taylor's series and Maclaurin's series expansion of one variable. |
| 2 | Newton-Raphson method — derivation and geometrical interpretation. Problems. |
| 3 | Taylor's series and Maclaurin's series expansion of two variables. |
| 4 | Solution of the system of nonlinear equations using Newton-Raphson method. |
| 5 | Maxima and minima of functions of two variables. |
| 6 | Lagrange's method of undetermined multipliers. |

## UNIT-II — First Order and First Degree Differential Equations & Numerical Solution of First Order ODE
*Sessions 7–11 · 5 hrs*

| Session | Topic |
|---|---|
| 7 | Applications of ODEs to solve LR, RC circuits and Newton's law of cooling. |
| 8 | Orthogonal trajectories. |
| 9 | Numerical solution of ODEs using Taylor's series method. |
| 10 | Euler's and modified Euler's method. |
| 11 | Runge-Kutta method of fourth order. |

## UNIT-III — Linear Differential Equations of Higher Order
*Sessions 12–17 · 6 hrs*

| Session | Topic |
|---|---|
| 12 | Introduction to LDE with constant coefficients. Solution of homogeneous LDE with constant coefficients. |
| 13 | Method of finding Particular Integral for standard cases. |
| 14 | Problems continued on L13. |
| 15 | Cauchy's and Legendre's linear differential equations. |
| 16 | Solution of initial and boundary value problems. |
| 17 | Method of variation of parameters. |

## UNIT-IV — Interpolation, Numerical Differentiation & Numerical Integration
*Sessions 18–23 · 6 hrs*

| Session | Topic |
|---|---|
| 18 | Finite differences. |
| 19 | Newton-Gregory forward and backward difference interpolation. |
| 20 | Lagrange's interpolation. Newton's divided difference interpolation. |
| 21 | Numerical differentiation. |
| 22 | Newton-Cotes quadrature formula. Trapezoidal rule. |
| 23 | Simpson's 1/3rd and 3/8th rule. |

## UNIT-V — Linear Algebra
*Sessions 24–28 · 5 hrs*

| Session | Topic |
|---|---|
| 24 | Elementary transformations on a matrix, Echelon form & rank of a matrix. Consistency of system of linear equations. |
| 25 | Gauss elimination method & Gauss-Seidel method to solve the system of linear equations. |
| 26 | Eigen values and eigen vectors of a matrix. |
| 27 | Diagonalization of square matrices. Rayleigh's power method. |
| 28 | Solution of system of ODEs using matrix method. |
